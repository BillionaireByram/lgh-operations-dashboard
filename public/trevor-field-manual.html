Section 05 · The three AI roles
FM-001 // ROSTER

Each agent has one job.

AGT-01
Commander Chief of Staff

Tracks the whole system, reports what matters, catches broken data, and turns noise into one clear priority.

“Give me today’s revenue report.”
AGT-02
Ads Commander Traffic Operator

Uses Pipeboard only to judge campaigns, spend, cost per lead, and real webinar sign-up quality.

“Which ad is creating webinar registrations?”
AGT-03
Strategy Commander Pipeline Operator

Segments leads, updates tags, queues follow-up, and pushes the hottest leads toward booked calls.

“Who is closest to buying right now?”

Section 06 · Day to day
FM-001 // RHYTHM

The daily operating rhythm.

The rule.

Do not start the day inside tools. Start with the dashboard, then ask Commander what needs action.

Dashboard first. Commander second. GHL, Pipeboard, and calls only when the dashboard points there.
Morning · 0600
Read the scoreboard. Check leads, booked calls, deals, cash, and health.
Midday · 1200
Work the bottleneck. If leads are low, review ads. If show rate is low, push watch follow-up. If sales is low, review hot leads.
Evening · 1800
Close the loop. Ask what changed today and what one move matters tomorrow.

Section 07 · Commander prompts
FM-001 // PROMPTS

Use Commander like a chief of staff.

commander // session 24-04
Give me today’s revenue report in plain English.
Are ads, webinar, calls, and deals tracking correctly?
Where are we leaking the most money right now?
What should Trevor focus on today?
Which leads need human attention right now?
Expected answer

Situation, impact, fix, decision.

Commander should give the situation, the business impact, the fix it can take, and the one decision Trevor may need to make. It should not punt problems to someone else.

Best outcome: Trevor sees the issue, the fix, and the next revenue move in under 60 seconds.

Section 08 · Ads Commander
FM-001 // ADS

Traffic is judged by buyers, not clicks.

Owns

What it watches

  • Pipeboard spend and campaign data.
  • Cost per webinar sign-up.
  • Campaigns that create booked calls.
  • Creative hooks worth testing next.
Ask it this

Daily prompts

“What should we kill, keep, or scale?”
“Which ad creates real webinar registrations?”
“Give me 3 new hooks based on the winner.”
Rule: Ads Commander uses Pipeboard only for ad performance. The goal is cheaper qualified attention that turns into webinar movement.

Section 11 · Closed-loop intelligence
FM-001 // WKLY

The weekly report should create action.

Reviews

What the weekly loop checks

  • Ad source to webinar sign-up.
  • Registration to attendance.
  • Watch behavior to booked calls.
  • Booked calls to shows and deals.
  • Revenue by campaign and source.
Expectation

What Trevor should expect

Not a list of chores. The report should show the leak, what the system can fix, what it proposes to fix next, and what decision only Trevor can make.

“What did the learning loop find, and what can you fix this week?”

Section 12 · How to change the system
FM-001 // LEVERS

Change one lever at a time.

If traffic is weak

Tune the front door.

Ask Ads Commander for the top winner, the worst loser, and three new hooks. Change creative or budget.

If show rate is weak

Tighten the watch.

Ask Strategy Commander for not-watched and partial-watch leads. Tighten reminders and follow-up pressure.

If sales is weak

Sharpen the close.

Ask Commander which hot leads did not close and what objection pattern is showing up.

The operating question

Every day: what one move makes the company more money today?
